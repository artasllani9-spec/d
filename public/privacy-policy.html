Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

ValueDex is an Adopt Me! value and trading tool. This policy explains what we collect when you use the website and Discord bot, and how that information is used.

1. What ValueDex is

ValueDex provides USD-based pet and item values, a trade calculator, a trading board for posting and accepting offers, profiles, and Discord commands that look up or update values. It is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or Adopt Me!

2. What we collect

When you browse without logging in, we may collect basic technical information such as pages viewed, device or browser type, and general usage patterns through standard hosting and analytics tools.

If you log in with Roblox, we receive the account information Roblox shares for authentication (such as your Roblox user ID, username, and profile thumbnail) so we can keep you signed in and show your profile on the site.

If you use trading features, we store trade listings and related activity you create (for example pets offered, acceptances, and completion or failure marks tied to your Roblox account).

If you use the ValueDex Discord bot in our server, Discord provides the information needed to run slash commands (such as your Discord user ID and the channel where a command was used). Value edits made through the bot are saved to our value database.

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask for your Roblox password. We do not sell personal data. We do not use your information for advertising networks or cross-site marketing profiles.

4. How data is used

We use this information to run ValueDex: show values, power the calculator, operate the trading board, keep accounts signed in, moderate abuse, improve the site, and sync value updates from Discord editors to the website.

5. Cookies and local storage

We may use cookies or local storage for login sessions and basic preferences. These are not used for third-party advertising.

6. Sharing

We may share data with infrastructure providers that host ValueDex (for example web hosting, databases, or Discord’s API) only as needed to operate the service. Trade listings and public profile details you choose to show may be visible to other ValueDex users.

7. Your choices

You can browse much of the site without logging in. You can clear cookies or site data in your browser. To remove account-linked trade history or ask privacy questions, contact us through Discord.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ValueDex grows. Continued use after changes are posted means you accept the update.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through our Discord server at https://discord.gg/QmQgcTkvTE.
