FAQ Guide

Frequently asked questions

How values work and what you need to trade.

How are ValueDex values calculated?

Values are shown in USD and maintained by the ValueDex team. Editors watch active trading and update pets and items through the site and Discord bot so the list stays close to what people are actually trading for.

What do Fly, Ride, Neon, and Mega mean on the value list?

Those are Adopt Me! potion and neon states. Pets can have different USD values for FR, NFR, and MFR. The value list and calculator use the state you select so you are comparing the right version of the pet.

How often are values updated?

The market moves quickly. The team updates values regularly, and bigger swings during events or demand spikes can be pushed the same day through Discord editor commands that sync to the website.

Is ValueDex affiliated with Roblox or Adopt Me!?

No. ValueDex is an independent community tool. Values and trades are for guidance only and do not guarantee what you will get in-game.

Do I need to log in?

You can browse values and use the calculator without an account. Logging in with Discord is required to post trades, accept offers, and use profile-linked trading features.
